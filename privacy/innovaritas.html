Innovaritas Website Privacy Policy

This policy covers your use of innovaritas.com and any message you send us through it. Our products and offerings have their own privacy policies, listed on the privacy policies page, which cover how those products and offerings handle information.

Who we are

This site is run by Innovaritas LLC, a limited liability company in Virginia, United States. You can reach us through the contact form.

What we collect and why

If you use the contact form, we receive the information you enter in its fields. We use it only to reply to you. The form also contains a hidden field meant to catch automated spam, which collects nothing from real visitors.

Our hosting provider may record technical details such as your IP address when pages load.

Cookies and tracking

This site loads its fonts from its own server. We do not use cookies, analytics, or advertising tools, the site does not store information in your browser, and we do not track you across other sites. Because we do not track visitors, there is nothing for a "Do Not Track" signal to turn off.

Who receives your information

Our hosting provider hosts this site and stores contact form submissions. Submissions may be automatically checked for spam by the hosting provider or a third-party spam-filtering service. We do not sell your information.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep contact form messages only as long as we need them to reply and keep reasonable records, then we delete them. To ask us to correct or delete your information, send a message through the contact form.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this page and the date below.
